TheoryPrep

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information TheoryPrep collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

TheoryPrep is run by TheoryPrep, United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR). Contact us about your data at theoryprep.uk@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: an anonymous user ID, created when you first open the app.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Learning progress: your answers, test results, scores, streak, XP, badges, test date and study plan.
  • Purchases: whether you have Premium, and the store's purchase records, such as the product, price, date, country and transaction ID. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Referrals: your invite code, which friends joined with it and whether they've started learning, and whose code you used, if any. People you invite only see counts, never your progress, and you never see theirs.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device, if you allow notifications.
  • Technical: your device type, operating system and app version, and your IP address when the app connects to our services.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts or photos. Read Aloud uses your phone's own voice, so the text stays on your phone.

3. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run the app: save your progress, sync it across your phones, build your study plan and send the reminders you've turned on. Legal basis: contract, because this is the service you've asked for.
  • To sell and provide Premium: check your purchase, unlock Premium and restore it. Legal basis: contract.
  • To run invites: link you to the friend who invited you, and reward you both. Legal basis: legitimate interests.
  • To keep sales records for tax. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep the app working and secure, and to improve it. Legal basis: legitimate interests, which we balance against your rights.

Notifications are optional. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We use these companies to run TheoryPrep. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase: accounts, sign-in, data storage and hosting.
  • RevenueCat: checking purchases and managing Premium. It receives your anonymous user ID and your purchase records.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): taking payment and processing refunds, under their own privacy policies.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: delivering push notifications.

Explanations and tips written with AI were made in advance. We don't send your data to an AI service.

We may share data if the law requires it. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. International transfers

Some of these companies are based in, or store data in, the United States. When data leaves the UK, we rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and progress: until you delete your account. We may delete anonymous accounts that haven't been used for two years.
  • Purchase records: for six years after the purchase, as UK tax law requires.
  • Push tokens: until you turn notifications off, delete your account, or the token stops working.

Deleting your account in Settings deletes your account, progress, invite code and the record of who invited you straight away. Friends you invited keep their own records, which no longer link to you. Your purchase records stay with Apple or Google, and with RevenueCat for our tax records, but are no longer linked to your progress. To have your RevenueCat record deleted too, email us.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data,
  • have it corrected,
  • have it deleted,
  • object to, or restrict, how we use it,
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere, and
  • withdraw your consent where we rely on it, such as for notifications.

To use any of these rights, email theoryprep.uk@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, which protects it with industry-standard security. The app's security rules stop anyone else's account reading your progress.

9. Children

TheoryPrep is made for learner drivers, who can take the theory test from 16. It isn't aimed at younger children, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before buying Premium.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when what we collect or how we use it changes, and show the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll tell you in the app.
